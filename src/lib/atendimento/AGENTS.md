# Attendance server and domain rules
- WhatsApp display names use owner workspace `agendaWhatsApp`, official UltraMsg `isMyContact/name` provenance, country-aware phone keys and CAS merges; monitor polling refreshes the authorized entry owner's names; photos come only from UltraMsg `contacts/image` by exact chat id, refreshed daily, initials fallback. Legal case names and identity/approval context are never rewritten. Why: phone labels are presentation, not legal identity.

- Anne books and consults recorded process facts through the reviewed server flow; `limparForaEscopoAna` strips investigative questions, internal agenda rules and evaluation/referral promises; domestic violence keeps internal URGENT flag, short acknowledgement leading to real slots, safety question only on stated imminent risk, never spontaneous 190. Why: factual consultation does not authorize legal assessment.

- Intake defaults to automatic, preserving modes/pauses. Active `ana-prompt.txt` and `ana-restrictions.txt` supply identity with owner training; `ana-scheduling.ts` validates intent, offers and pending-slot consent. Why: no forced greeting or unsafe booking.
- Acquisition requests and contextual acceptance of a consultation invitation use shared `ana-rules.ts` policies in production and simulator; preserve selected-slot/name booking and the official ProJuris agenda. Why: prevent model-invented handoffs and repeated consent without bypassing legal or human controls.
- Ana inference uses `ana.functions.ts` with the uploaded `claudeComoSample` adapter; UltraMsg uses channel-validated orchestration, consent and released conversation-owned destinations. Telegram is retired and production restrictions apply before incoming processing and outgoing provider calls. Why: preserve operational safety and provider billing.
- Ana books, reschedules and cancels; process news uses the shared approval flow through server ports, while meaning, documents, contract, payment and legal advice stay team-only; `processo` intent still falls back to draft. Fictional slots are never stored as offers. Why: permit reviewed news without weakening legal or scheduling gates.
- Scheduling wallet is classified by `classificarAgenda` (intake-rules.ts): staff confirmation > active bond in the synced local base by principal phone > self-declaration > prior context > new-lead default. Active clients book Fridays only; new leads Monday–Thursday. Duplicate phone in the base is never self-classified — it goes to the team; cadastro-confirmed classification is persisted on the conversation. Why: exclusive days per client type without guessing bonds from conversation history.
- Slot offers use `parManhaTardeAna` (nearest morning + nearest afternoon); the initial deterministic greeting queries Calendar first and persists that offer. A choice without a name is held in `horarioPendente` and the full name is asked; the name then books that slot with revalidation, accepted while the slot is still in the future even if another message came in between, and a pending choice never transfers to the team. The client name is stripped before the ethics filter in `aplicarRegrasAna` ("Vitória" is a name, not "chance de vitória"). Choices are parsed after `normalizarEscolhaHorario` ("As9:30", "9 e meia"); a name arriving right after the name request recovers an unsaved choice from the previous client message, and Anne never claims a choice was noted unless it was persisted. Existing conversations, modes, pauses and human requests remain protected. Why: exact initial flow without invented availability or lost choices.
- Process news uses shared `consulta-processo.ts` in server/simulator: principal-phone identity is revalidated against the complete provider index; staff-confirmed CPF links remain valid; official ownership/privacy/status are rechecked and stale snapshots refresh via existing owner leases/append-only commits. Lists contain only public linked processes and selection is activated only after approved, confirmed delivery; updates also require reviewer hash approval. Recorded factual questions consult, while meaning/strategy, files, money, mutations and third parties stay human. Why: safe current facts without unreviewed disclosure or arbitrary process selection.
- Ana test battery: scenarios in `tools/simulador-ana/bateria/cenarios.ts` run on every suite (`src/test/ana-bateria-regressao.test.ts`, simulated classification) and on the "Bateria de testes da Ana" page with the real model and a 12-criteria evaluator. Add a scenario for every new rule or fixed bug. Why: change, automatic test, report, fix, retest without manual chats.
- Identified returns share `ana-retomada.ts` across production and simulator, append greeting and continuation through one verified send and never reset legal or scheduling context; identification also counts names found only in the persisted history (`nomeConhecido`), never the last-message date. Why: avoid duplicate sends and automatic onboarding on return.
- `historicoContato` sorts persisted messages chronologically by `dataHora` (stable), so out-of-order webhook deliveries never scramble Anne's context; the webhook persists the message before `processarAna` reads the fresh row. Why: continuity must come from the database, never from an open interface.
- Message deletion preserves original text and response replay markers, hides per user or tombstones only after verified provider acknowledgement; no calendar cancellation is implied. Why: keep audit evidence and avoid false deletion claims.
- Production recovery uses message JSON execution claims and authenticated managed scheduling; retry only pre-effect transient generation, never ambiguous sends or calendar changes. Human control revisions and explicit send reservations guard final UltraMsg calls and echo recognition. Why: durable browser-independent recovery without sending over staff.
- Ana health monitoring runs from a managed 5-minute schedule into `ana_monitoramento` with transition-only alerts in audit_log; Claude calls reserve a daily budget atomically via service-role database functions and per-conversation reply bursts are blocked. Why: 24/7 observability and spend/loop limits without browser timers.
- Day-of reminders (`lembrete-consulta.server.ts`, official template 01) run inside the 5-minute monitor between 08:00 and 12:00 São Paulo, never within 30 minutes of the start, reserved before sending and never resent; no cancellation consent is stored (NÃO goes to rescheduling, never cancels). Why: no duplicate or late reminders and no unauthorized cancellations.
- Daily report (`relatorio-diario.server.ts`) runs inside the monitor between 08:00 and 10:00 São Paulo and summarizes the previous day per client into priority / scheduling-only / in-progress, reserved once per day in `ana_monitoramento`. Why: one report, never duplicated.
- One managed schedule (`monitorar-ana`, every 5 min) runs every operational automation through `monitor-operacional.server.ts`: isolated, idempotent steps within a ~45 s budget, state in `ana_monitoramento` id `monitor-operacional` (pure shape in `monitor-estado.ts`, counts only, no client data), audit only on step transitions, stale (>15 min) alert in Atendimento; the old routine routes still answer but need no schedule. Why: browser-independent automations with visible health (see docs/automacoes-operacionais.md).
- ProJuris agenda sync (`sincronizacao-agenda.ts` pure): consultations made directly in ProJuris link only by the client's phone in title/description or the full name of a confirmed ProJuris bond, with a single candidate (else a panel pendency); receipt = official code `pj:<codigo>` + read times (`origem: 'projuris_sincronizado'`); time changes follow ProJuris; a missing compromisso is cancelled only after `situacaoCompromisso` confirms concluded/absent, never on provider error. Why: no invented receipts or cross-client links.
- Consultation situation (`situacao-consulta.ts`): agendada/confirmada/realizada/ausente/cancelada; realizada and ausência only by authenticated staff record (`registrarSituacaoConsultaAnne`), never because time passed; one team alert when a past consultation lacks a record; ausência starts automatic rescheduling. Why: no presumed outcomes.
- Incoming WhatsApp audio/voice (audio, ptt) is stored for staff but never starts intake or reaches Anne; `processarAna` returns early for audio. Why: the office handles voice messages manually.
- `ana-relacionamento` separates acquisition eligibility from agenda wallet using verified phone, active client involvement, same-contact contract/step and persisted history; the final offer/reservation path blocks automatic acquisition for existing/uncertain clients. Why: media or reopened chats never erase the relationship, and process disclosures retain their review gates.
- Paid international funnel lives in `funil-internacional.ts` (pure, tested) and runs before the normal Anne flow until staff confirm payment via `confirmarPagamentoFunil`. Why: paid flow must never leak into free intake.
- Agenda: ProJuris is always tried first; on ProJuris failure Anne reads Google Calendar busy times and books a provisional Google event (`pendenteProjuris`), which the 5-minute monitor posts to ProJuris idempotently by codigoExterno. Why: Anne must keep answering clients while ProJuris stays the primary agenda.
- Office actions with simulated stubs (`call()` in integracoes.ts) refuse to run unless the channel is explicitly `desligado` (`trava-simulacao.ts`). Why: demo links or fake bookings must never reach a real client.
- Presence confirmation (`confirmacao-consulta.ts` pure, `.server.ts` in the monitor): official library text, sent once per `inicio` on the first cycle within the configured lead (`secretaria.confirmacaoAntecedenciaMin`, default 120, 30–1440) and more than 30 minutes before; skipped when presence is already confirmed, the reminder went out <90 min ago, staff spoke about it in the last 3 h, or the situation is realizada/ausente/cancelada; reserved in `agenteAna.confirmacao40` (legacy name) before sending; ≤30 min, human control with the secretary off, pause, Desligada, no link or no receipt only create a team alert (`avisosEquipe`). Replies count only while the confirmation is the last office message: "ok" confirms presence; "não/atrasar/remarcar" never cancels — with the secretary on and real agenda slots Anne starts automatic rescheduling (`iniciarReagendamentoAutomatico`: official 06 with two slots today, else the approved slot list; choice → reserve-new-before-conclude with receipt), otherwise the explicit reschedule/cancel question plus a priority alert. Why: confirm consultations without duplicate sends, unauthorized cancellations or speaking over staff.
- Controlled test mode (`secretaria.testeAutomacoes` {ativo, numero}, `bloqueadoPorTeste`): when on, operational automations (motor `modo_teste`, reminder and confirmation routines) only reach the authorized number. Why: real tests without messaging real clients.
- Attendance actions never fake execution: Trato feito keeps the contract pending (ZapSign off → ProJuris link sent through the channel, `contrato:'enviado'` only after a real send); signing reuses/creates the real Drive folder; staff slots/bookings use `agenda-equipe.functions.ts` (official agenda, server-side receipt + client notice); Meet only from the receipt's HTTPS link; charges show "não configurado"; staff attachments stay "Guardado (não enviado ao cliente)" until `enviarAnexoCanal` gets an UltraMsg receipt (10-minute signed Storage URL, reserved before sending). Why: no demo links, false "sent" states or stale browser saves undoing receipts.
- Collection concierge (`coleta.ts` pure, `coleta.server.ts` routine every 30 min): after Trato feito, Anne acknowledges an attachment only with the server storage receipt (`path` + `salvoStart`) and lists missing items by real `checklist_itens` names; document evaluation questions go to staff. Contract (24 h after real send) and missing-item (48 h) reminders run on business days 9:00–17:30, max 3 per pendency (D3) then a team alert; Sugerir/human control/pause produce ready-text alerts; proposed texts are editable quick replies read server-side. Staff proof lists add checklist items only after a real send. Why: persistent follow-up without false receipts, endless nagging or document assessment by the agent.
- Follow-up concierge (`acompanhamento.ts` pure, `.server.ts`): after a client-requested process news is approved and delivered, one proposed closing question (D4, editable quick reply) is sent once per request; an explicit "sim" while it is the last office message offers Friday return slots through the normal agenda flow, "não/esclareceu" ends it silently. Hearing/expert-exam movements from a manual ProJuris sync become reviewer-queue drafts (`PedidoProcesso.evento`) only for confirmed-link clients, built from the official /audiencia and /pericia templates with fields read only from the record (missing fields removed; movement date never used as event date), deduped by process|type|date|time, never for private movements or secret cases, never generating "em conferência" client notices, and never blocking new client requests. Why: proactive follow-up without unreviewed disclosure, invented facts or ProJuris writes.
- Conversation list loads once (`consultaCasos`, staleTime Infinity) and `useCasosRealtime` applies only the changed row (`sincronizacao.ts`), with a changed-rows sweep and full reload only on reconnect; never add polling or full-list reloads after saves. Team texts go through `enviarMensagemEquipe` (reserve by browser id → `enviarComRegras` → receipt; ambiguous provider failures need confirmation) via the per-conversation `fila-envio.ts`. Receipts come from the `message_create` echo id (`recibos-whatsapp.ts`); the webhook base URL comes from `url-publica.ts`. Why: WhatsApp-Web speed without duplicate sends, lost messages or weaker gates (see docs/atendimento-whatsapp-desempenho.md).
- Team alerts (`avisos-equipe.ts`) live in case JSON with deterministic ids, merge by id on browser saves and feed the Assessor; ready texts are only inserted into the composer, never sent automatically. Why: server routines can ask staff for action without schema changes or silent sends.
- Official message library (`biblioteca/`, 19 CS Lawyers templates): texts are deterministic and never rewritten by the model; only bracketed fields are filled with verified data (`renderizarOficial` blocks on any missing/invalid field or inactive template) and `selecao.ts` checks context compatibility ("Bom dia" only mornings, "Hoje"/"amanhã" only with real slots that day, 08.1 only without a Meet link to deliver) falling back to the previously approved text or to a team alert; 01/02/08.2 follow `politicaConsultaDia` (one reminder and one confirmation per consultation). New versions, activation and the paid-consultation choice are senior/admin server writes with history; snapshots in `src/test/__snapshots__/` guard the official text. Why: standardized communication exactly as approved by the office (see docs/biblioteca-mensagens-oficiais.md).
- Commercial filter (`filtro-comercial.ts`, shared by `processarAna` and the simulator) runs after the short-reply rule and before any model call: deterministic intent scoring marks ads, courses, subscriptions, prospecting and other companies' bots as `comercial` (no reply, no intake/agenda/follow-up, kept in history, out of the reply queue) or `duvida` (no reply, silent `triagem` team alert); client-intent signals or a known contact never get discarded automatically. Why: no answers to spam or bot loops without ignoring people seeking help about offers, contracts, plans or charges.
- Digital secretary (`secretaria/`, flag `office_workspace.dados.secretaria.ativa`, on by default since 26/11/2026 — only explicit `false` disables; server kill switch `ANNE_SECRETARIA_DESLIGADA=1`): effective mode is the most restrictive of the stored mode and the legacy `humano`/`pausa`/`modoIA`/`erro` fields, so staff takeover turns Automático into Híbrido, Humano/Bloqueado are never overwritten and nothing returns to Automático without staff or the optional configured rule. Outside Automático only `operacional.ts` acts (deterministic replies to one active automation whose message is the last office message; rescheduling — client "não", staff-recorded absence or delegation — confirms only with the agenda receipt and never in Humano/Bloqueado); in Automático it only handles replies to an active rescheduling automation; confirmation/reminder routines ask `motor.ts`; automation sends reserve `secretaria.envio` and `conferirReservaEnvio` rechecks it right before UltraMsg. Config is written only by senior/admin server functions and preserved by `saveOffice`; every new rule gets a test in `src/test/anne-secretaria-*`. Why: Anne keeps authorized operational work under human control without speaking over staff, and turning the flag off restores the previous flow (see docs/anne-secretaria-digital.md).
